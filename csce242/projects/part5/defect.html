The
Defect

A mysterious automaton that channels Orbs to unleash lightning, frost, and powerful elemental combinations.

Starting HP: 75

Starting Relic: Cracked Core

Defect Relics

Defect Collection

Cracked Core

At the start of combat, Channel 1 Lightning.

Data Disk

Start each combat with 1 Focus.

Gold-Plated Cables

Your rightmost Orb triggers its passive one additional time.

Symbiotic Virus

At the start of combat, Channel 1 Dark.

Emotion Chip

Whenever you lose HP, Channel 1 Lightning.

Frozen Core

Replaces Cracked Core. Start combat with 1 Frost.

Inserter

Every 2 turns, gain 1 Orb slot.

Nuclear Battery

At the start of combat, Channel 1 Plasma.

Runic Capacitor

Start each combat with 3 additional Orb slots.

Starting Deck

The Defect begins with 4 Strikes, 4 Defends, Zap, and Dualcast.
